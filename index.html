Do first: book the three rifugio nights (Lagazuoi → Fanes/Lavarella → Sennes) this week — late September is the final stretch of the hut season and beds go fast. Everything else can wait a few days.

The plan, phase by phase

Click a peak on the elevation profile above, or scroll. Holidays are built in: Yom Kippur falls on the last Cyprus day, Sukkot starts the night before the hut trek, Simchat Torah lands on the Ortisei rest day.

Phase 0 · Solo

Cyprus warm-up

Thu 17 → Mon 21 Sep · fly LCA→VCE Tue 22

Your weekend before the crew assembles.

  • Troodos Mountains hike (training day)
  • Akamas / Blue Lagoon or Cape Greco
  • Erev Yom Kippur Sun 20/9 · fast ends Mon night
Phase 1 · All three

Cortina d'Ampezzo

Tue 22 → Fri 25 Sep · 3 nights

Meet at Venice airport, drive up, straight into the icons.

  • Tre Cime di Lavaredo loop (leave 6:30!)
  • Lago di Sorapis turquoise lake
  • Cinque Torri light day + pack hut bags
Phase 2 · The centerpiece

Hut-to-hut on Alta Via 1

Sat 26 → Tue 29 Sep · 3 hut nights

Lago di Braies → Fanes plateau → sleep at 2,752 m.

  • N1: Rifugio Sennes or Biella
  • N2: Rifugio Fanes / Lavarella (brewery!)
  • N3: Rifugio Lagazuoi — sunrise of the trip
Phase 3

Val Gardena (Ortisei)

Tue 29 Sep → Sun 4 Oct · 5-6 nights

Second base. Laundry, sauna, big-view day hikes.

  • Seceda ridgeline (check lift dates)
  • Alpe di Siusi meadows · Sassolungo circuit
  • Simchat Torah rest day Sat 3/10
Phase 4 · Wind-down

Val di Funes → Lake Garda

Sun 4 → Fri 9 Oct

October-proof: lower, warmer, still gorgeous.

  • Adolf Munkel trail + Santa Maddalena
  • Bolzano & Ötzi museum en route south
  • Ponale trail, Monte Baldo, wine, lake
Finale

Venice send-off

Fri 9 → Sat 10 Oct

Drive down, golden-hour wander, final dinner, fly home.

  • Sleep near airport (Mestre is cheaper)
  • Return rental car at VCE
